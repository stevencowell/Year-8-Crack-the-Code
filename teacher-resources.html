Current Stage 4 syllabus mapping

TE4-DIG-02 is the central outcome: students use data and digital systems to code, design and produce a project. The course also addresses systems and components, planning and production, communication and evaluation, safe technology use and responsible digital interaction.

NESA outcome wording is authoritative. School programming and assessment decisions remain local.
