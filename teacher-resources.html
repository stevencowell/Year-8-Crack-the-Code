Current Stage 4 syllabus mapping

TE4-DIG-02 is the central outcome: students use data and digital systems to code, design and produce a project. The course also addresses systems and components, planning and production, communication and evaluation, safe technology use and responsible digital interaction.

NESA outcome wording is authoritative. School programming and assessment decisions remain local.

Evidence, feedback and access

  • Use every named knowledge check diagnostically and return students to the exact theory section when required.
  • Review saved scaffolded responses, folio entries and authentic practical/investigation evidence.
  • Use chunking, read-aloud, reduced-copy, keyboard and print/PDF options where appropriate.
  • Device-local autosave is not submission; current submission instructions remain Teacher to confirm.

Teacher to confirm before use

  1. Actual timetable and period placement.
  2. Every practical/fieldwork method, equipment, quantities, WHS controls, supervision and checkpoint.
  3. Current assessment status, dates, marks, rubric, adjustments and submission route.
  4. Faculty review and sign-off before the program is represented as approved.
